Anvil Support

Anvil (Anvil: Workout Tracker on the App Store) is a no-nonsense strength training log for iPhone and Android. It tracks your workout programs, sessions, sets, reps, and weights, with an optional AI coach, powered by Anthropic's Claude, that can build or adjust your program. The next-step suggestions you see during a workout are worked out on your phone and do not use AI.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my workout data stored?
On your device. Your programs, sessions, sets, reps, and weights are stored locally and are not uploaded anywhere by us. No account is required to use the core app. On iPhone, iCloud can keep a copy under your Apple ID. On Android, your phone's own backup can keep a copy in your Google Account. Both are yours to turn off, and the Privacy Policy explains each.
Do I need to sign in?
No, not for the core app. Signing in (with Apple on iPhone, with Google on Android, or as an anonymous guest on either) is only needed to use the optional AI coach. Before anything is sent to Anthropic's Claude the app asks for your agreement, and the Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent.
How do I delete my account and data?
In the app, open Settings and choose Delete account (on Android, it is in the Account section of Settings; on iPhone, Settings, then Account). It removes your account and every Coach request stored with it. The page How to delete your Anvil account has the steps, what is removed and what is not, and how to ask by email. Your on-device workout history is controlled by you through the app and your device, regardless of account status.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Email vandervalkjoel@gmail.com with a description of what happened, the steps to reproduce it if you can, and your device model and iOS or Android version. Screenshots help a lot.
I have a feature request or general feedback.
Send it to vandervalkjoel@gmail.com. Anvil is built and maintained by a single indie developer, so replies may take a little while, but every message is read.
