Anvil Support

Anvil: Workout Tracker is a no-nonsense strength training log for iPhone. It tracks your workout programs, sessions, sets, reps, and weights, with optional AI features to help generate a program or coach your progression on a lift.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my workout data stored?
On your device. Your programs, sessions, sets, reps, and weights are stored locally and are not uploaded anywhere by us. No account is required to use the core app.
Do I need to sign in?
No, not for the core app. Signing in (with Apple, or as an anonymous guest) is only needed to use the optional AI workout generator or AI progression coach. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is sent when you use those features.
How do I delete my account and data?
Open Settings in the app and choose the account deletion option to remove your account and any associated data stored on our backend. You can also email vandervalkjoel@gmail.com and we will delete it for you. Your on-device workout history is controlled by you through the app and your device, regardless of account status.
I found a bug. How do I report it?
Email vandervalkjoel@gmail.com with a description of what happened, the steps to reproduce it if you can, and your device model and iOS version. Screenshots help a lot.
I have a feature request or general feedback.
Send it to vandervalkjoel@gmail.com. Anvil is built and maintained by a single indie developer, so replies may take a little while, but every message is read.
