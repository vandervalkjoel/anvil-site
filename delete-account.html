How to delete your Anvil account

Anvil: Workout Tracker (iPhone) and Anvil (Android), by Joel Vandervalk. Last updated November 9, 2026.

You only have an Anvil account if you signed in to use the optional AI Coach. This page explains how to delete that account and the data stored with it. It does not apply to the workouts on your phone, which never leave your device unless you use your phone's backup (see "What is not deleted" below).

Delete it in the app (fastest)

Android

  1. Open Anvil and go to Settings.
  2. In the Account section, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Anvil deletes your account and signs you out.

iPhone

  1. Open Anvil and go to Settings, then Account.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Anvil deletes your account and signs you out.

Deleting a guest account has to happen this way, in the app. A guest account has no name or email, so once you sign out, or on Android uninstall the app, it can no longer be reached from your phone and we cannot match it to you. On Android, the same happens if you clear the app's storage or move to a new phone (a backup brings your workouts back but not your sign-in), so delete the account first; uninstalling does not delete it. On iPhone, uninstalling does not sign you out, so if you removed Anvil and have not signed out, reinstall it and delete the account from Settings, then Account.

Ask by email

Deleting it in the app is the only route for a guest account, which has no email address for us to match. If you cannot open the app, and your account has an email address (you signed in with Apple), email vandervalkjoel@gmail.com from that address with the subject "Delete my Anvil account" and say which app you used. We will delete the account and its data promptly, and reply when it is done. You do not need to reinstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your Anvil account on our backend (Supabase), including the name and email address attached to it if you shared them.
  • Every Coach request stored under your account: the text you typed, the program and equipment details that were sent with it, any photo or document you attached, and the answer that came back.
  • On the phone, Anvil also clears its stored sign-in, your Coach conversations and your Coach agreement.

What is not deleted

  • Your workouts on your phone. Programs, history, sets, reps and weights stay on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Your own backup copy. The iCloud copy on iPhone, or the Google Account backup on Android, is held by Apple or Google under your account. Delete it in your phone's settings (see the Privacy Policy).
  • Anonymous usage records. For each Coach call the backend keeps the model used, token counts, timing, the outcome and a fingerprint of the request. It holds none of your text, program or attachment, and deleting your account cuts its link to you. These records have no set end date. How Anthropic handles Coach requests is described in the Privacy Policy.
