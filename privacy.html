Anvil Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Anvil (published as "Anvil: Workout Tracker") is a strength training log. This policy explains what data Anvil handles, where it goes, and your choices. Anvil does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Who we are

Anvil is an independent app developed by Joel Vandervalk. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact vandervalkjoel@gmail.com.

Your workouts stay in your control

Anvil's core functionality, your programs, workout history, sets, reps, and weights, is stored on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). No account is required to use the app, and this data is never uploaded to us.

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, Anvil also keeps a copy of this data in your own private iCloud database, and keeps your equipment and lift settings in iCloud's key-value storage, so a new phone gets your history back and your settings follow you. Both are stored under your Apple ID and encrypted by Apple; we cannot read either. You can turn iCloud off for Anvil, or delete what it stored, in your iPhone's Settings under your name, iCloud, then Anvil.

Optional AI features

Anvil has two optional AI features. Both are powered by Gemini, an artificial intelligence service operated by Google.

  • The Coach tab. You type a request about your training plan (build a program, change today's workout, adjust for travel) and the coach answers with a plan you can review, apply, or discard.
  • The in-workout briefing. When you start a workout, the coach suggests a weight and rep target for each lift in that session, based on your recent sets for those lifts.

Both are optional, and the rest of the app works fully without them. Nothing is sent to Google unless you use one of these features and have agreed to it first.

Signing in

Using either feature requires an account on our backend, so that your requests belong to you and can be deleted with you. You can:

  • Sign in with Apple. You can choose to share your name and email with us (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one, at your choice), or
  • Continue as guest. Anvil creates an anonymous account with no name or email attached.

Your permission comes first

The first time an AI feature is about to send anything, the app shows you what will be sent and who receives it, and asks for your agreement. Nothing is sent unless you agree. If you decline, the request is discarded and nothing leaves your phone, and you are asked again the next time rather than being opted in quietly. Signing out resets this answer, so the next person to sign in on the phone is asked for themselves.

What a Coach ask sends

  • the text you type;
  • the structure of your current program: its day names, the exercises on each day, and their set counts and rep ranges (not your logged sets);
  • which day is next in your rotation;
  • your equipment setup: the equipment you have checked off, whether you use a plate inventory, the settings of any plate-loaded machines, and, while a travel window is active, its end date and the equipment available there;
  • your weight increment;
  • the previous turns of the current conversation, up to the last ten, as text (attachments are never re-sent);
  • a photo, PDF, or plain-text document, only if you attach one; and
  • the app's built-in exercise catalog, which is not personal data.

What an in-workout briefing sends

  • the lifts in that session, with their target sets and rep ranges;
  • for each of those lifts, your recent completed sessions: the date, and the weight, reps, and set type of each set;
  • your weight increment for each lift;
  • the app's own next-step suggestion for each lift (an action, weight, and reps worked out from the sets above), which the coach explains rather than replaces; and
  • your equipment setup, as described above.

What is never sent to Google

Anvil never sends your name, your email address, your Apple ID, your account identifier, your device identifiers, or your location to Google, and sends no part of your training log beyond what is listed above. A document you choose to attach is sent as you provide it, so anything inside it (an author name in a PDF, for example) travels with it.

How Google handles it

We use Google's paid Gemini API service, under which Google does not use the prompts we send or the responses it returns to train or improve its products. We do not use your data to train any artificial intelligence model either. Google returns a plan or a set of weight and rep targets, which you can review, edit, apply, or discard in the app.

Where your data goes

  • Supabase hosts our backend: your account (if you sign in) and the AI requests described above.
  • Google operates the Gemini service that processes the content of each AI request to produce your plan or briefing.

We do not share your data with any other third parties. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Data retention and your choices

Each AI request, including any attachment and the answer that came back, is stored on our backend under your account so the feature can work, so we can limit abuse, and so we can troubleshoot failures. These records are kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and every request stored with it directly in the app, from Settings, or by emailing vandervalkjoel@gmail.com and we will remove it. Your on-device workout history is controlled entirely by you through the app and your device, independent of any account.

Children

Anvil is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
