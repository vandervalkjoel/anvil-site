Anvil Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Anvil (published as "Anvil: Workout Tracker") is a strength training log. This policy explains what data Anvil handles, where it goes, and your choices. Anvil does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Who we are

Anvil is an independent app developed by Joel Vandervalk. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact vandervalkjoel@gmail.com.

Your workouts stay in your control

Anvil's core functionality, your programs, workout history, sets, reps, and weights, is stored on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). No account is required to use the app, and this data is never uploaded to us.

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud, Anvil also keeps a copy of this data in your own private iCloud database, and keeps your equipment and lift settings in iCloud's key-value storage, so a new phone gets your history back and your settings follow you. Both are stored under your Apple ID and encrypted by Apple; we cannot read either. You can turn iCloud off for Anvil, or delete what it stored, in your iPhone's Settings under your name, iCloud, then Anvil.

If you turn on Gym arrival, Anvil saves your gym's location on your iPhone and, with your permission to use location Always, your iPhone wakes Anvil when you arrive there so it can show one notification a day. Anvil does not track where you go: it only learns that you arrived at the spot you saved. The location is not sent to us or to Anthropic, and is not synced to your other devices. If you choose your gym on the map, the gym map and its search use Apple Maps, which receives the area the map shows and any search text. You can remove your gym any time in Anvil's Settings.

The optional AI coach

Anvil has one optional AI feature, the Coach, which you reach from the Coach tab or from the Travel sheet. It is powered by Claude, an artificial intelligence service operated by Anthropic. You type a request about your training plan (build a program, change today's workout, adjust for travel) and the coach answers with a plan you can review, apply, or discard.

The Coach is optional, and the rest of the app works fully without it. Nothing is sent to Anthropic unless you use the Coach and have agreed to it first.

The in-workout briefing, which suggests a next step for each lift as you train, is worked out on your iPhone from your own logged sets. It does not use AI, needs no account, and sends nothing to us or to Anthropic.

Signing in

Using the Coach requires an account on our backend, so that your requests belong to you and can be deleted with you. You can:

  • Sign in with Apple. You can choose to share your name and email with us (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one, at your choice), or
  • Continue as guest. Anvil creates an anonymous account with no name or email attached.

Your permission comes first

The first time the Coach is about to send anything, the app shows you what will be sent and who receives it, and asks for your agreement. Nothing is sent unless you agree. If you decline, the request is discarded and nothing leaves your phone, and you are asked again the next time rather than being opted in quietly. Signing out resets this answer, so the next person to sign in on the phone is asked for themselves.

What a Coach ask sends

  • the text you type;
  • the structure of your current program: its name, its day names and focus, the exercises on each day and how they are grouped into supersets, and their set counts and rep ranges;
  • for each exercise in your program, the weight and reps of the heaviest set from the last session you logged it (not the rest of your training log);
  • which day is next in your rotation;
  • your equipment setup: the equipment you have checked off, whether you use a plate inventory, the settings of any plate-loaded machines, and the end date and equipment of a travel window, including one you are planning when you ask the Coach to adapt for travel;
  • your preferred weight unit (pounds or kilograms);
  • the previous turns of the current conversation, up to the last ten, as text (attachments are never re-sent);
  • a photo, PDF, or plain-text document, only if you attach one; and
  • the app's built-in exercise catalog, which is not personal data.

What is never sent to Anthropic

Anvil never sends your name, your email address, your Apple ID, your account identifier, your device identifiers, or your location to Anthropic, and sends no part of your training log beyond what is listed above. A document you choose to attach is sent as you provide it, so anything inside it (an author name in a PDF, for example) travels with it.

How Anthropic handles it

We use Anthropic's Claude API under its commercial terms, under which Anthropic may not train its models on the prompts we send or the responses it returns. We do not use your data to train any artificial intelligence model either. Claude returns a plan, which you can review, edit, apply, or discard in the app.

Where your data goes

  • Supabase hosts our backend: your account (if you sign in) and the AI requests described above.
  • Anthropic operates the Claude service that processes the content of each Coach request to produce your plan.
  • Apple Maps is used only when you open the gym map: it receives the area the map shows and any search text, sent straight from your iPhone to Apple, never through Anvil.

Supabase and Anthropic process this data only to provide their service to Anvil, under contractual data protection terms that give your data the same or equal protection described in this policy. Apple Maps is a direct connection from your iPhone, described above, and is not covered by those terms.

Apart from the services listed above, we do not share your data with any other third parties. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Data retention and your choices

Each AI request, including any attachment and the answer that came back, is stored on our backend under your account so the feature can work, so we can limit abuse, and so we can troubleshoot failures. These records are kept until you delete your account. You can delete your account and every request stored with it directly in the app, from Settings, or by emailing vandervalkjoel@gmail.com and we will remove it. Your on-device workout history is controlled entirely by you through the app and your device, independent of any account.

Children

Anvil is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
