Anvil Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2, 2026

Anvil (published as "Anvil: Workout Tracker") is a strength training log. This policy explains what data Anvil handles, where it goes, and your choices. Anvil does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Who we are

Anvil is an independent app developed by Joel Vandervalk. For any privacy question, or to request deletion of your data, contact vandervalkjoel@gmail.com.

Your workouts stay on your device

Anvil's core functionality, your programs, workout history, sets, reps, and weights, is stored entirely on your device using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). No account is required to use the app, and this data is never uploaded anywhere by us. If you use Apple's iCloud backup for your device, your workout data may be included in that backup, which is between you and Apple.

Optional AI features

Anvil includes two optional AI features: an AI workout generator and an AI progression coach. These are opt-in; the rest of the app works fully without them. Using either feature requires signing in:

  • Sign in with Apple. You can choose to share your name and email with us (Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your real one, at your choice), or
  • Continue as guest. Anvil creates an anonymous account with no name or email attached.

When you use an AI feature, we send the following to our backend to generate your result:

  • A summary of relevant workout data. For the AI workout generator, this is your stated goal, training days per week, session length, experience level, and available equipment. For the progression coach, this is a summary of your recent sets, reps, and weights for the exercise in question.
  • Any text you type. Free-text notes you enter (for example, equipment details or other context for the workout generator) are sent along with your request.
  • An optional photo. If you choose to attach a photo (for example, of your home gym equipment) when generating a workout, that photo is sent as part of the request. Photos are never required.

These requests are processed by our backend (Supabase) and by Anthropic's Claude API to produce your result. Requests are stored server-side so the service can operate and so we can rate-limit and troubleshoot it. This data is used solely to provide the AI features to you; it is not used for advertising and is not sold.

Where your data goes

  • Supabase hosts our backend: your account (if you sign in), and the AI requests described above.
  • Anthropic (Claude) processes the content of AI requests (workout summaries, your typed text, and any attached photo) to generate workout plans and progression suggestions.

We do not share your data with any other third parties. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Data retention and your choices

You can delete your account and the associated data stored on our backend directly in the app, from Settings. You can also request deletion by emailing vandervalkjoel@gmail.com and we will remove it. Your on-device workout history is controlled entirely by you through the app and your device, independent of any account.

Children

Anvil is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date.
